Akshi is a private domain portfolio companion for independent developers,
consultants, small teams, and anyone responsible for domains they do not want
to lose track of.

The name comes from the Sanskrit word akṣi (अक्षि), meaning “eye”. Its guiding
line is: “Watch what matters.”

Open Akshi to review domain registration and expiry information, inspect the
certificate presented by a TLS endpoint, browse DNS records, and examine
published email configuration including MX, SPF, DMARC, DKIM, MTA-STS, and
TLS-RPT.

Akshi performs these checks directly from your device and saves the latest
observations in private application storage. There is no Akshi account,
advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, cloud database, or Akshi-operated server.
Your portfolio and results are not sent to Aliyesha.

Akshi updates its information when you open or return to the app, refresh
manually, add a domain, or change its check configuration. It does not continuously
check websites while closed and is not an uptime or outage-alerting service.

Expiry reminders are enabled by default and can be turned off globally or for
individual domains. Akshi uses saved domain-registration and TLS-certificate
dates to schedule notifications on your device. Reminders occur only at your
selected warning points and the final one-day point. They are not daily alarms,
push notifications, or DNS and email change alerts.

Features:

• Registration and expiry information from public RDAP services
• Direct TLS certificate validity, trust, hostname, and fingerprint details
• A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, NS, TXT, and CAA records from the device DNS resolver
• Published MX, SPF, DMARC, DKIM, MTA-STS, and TLS-RPT configuration
• Attempt and verification times with retained evidence when a source fails
• On-device expiry reminders at configurable warning points
• Search, tags, attention views, local diagnostics, and light and dark themes
• No account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or Akshi server

Akshi reports public technical facts. Registry data can be incomplete, and
email checks do not provide a deliverability or reputation score. Akshi
complements registrar safeguards and dedicated uptime services; it does not
replace them.

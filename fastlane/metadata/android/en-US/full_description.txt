Akshi is a private, local-first monitor for domain registration, TLS
certificates, DNS records, and published email-domain configuration.

Choose the checks that matter for each domain. Akshi connects directly from
your device while the app is active, stores the latest observations locally,
and schedules consolidated expiry reminders through the operating system.

Features include:

• Domain registration dates and available registrar information through RDAP
• X.509 validity, trust, hostname, algorithm, and fingerprint details
• A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, NS, TXT, and CAA DNS snapshots
• MX, SPF, DMARC, MTA-STS, TLS-RPT, and explicit DKIM checks
• One daily reminder containing every domain that needs attention
• Local tags, cached website icons, and light and dark themes
• No account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or central Akshi server

Checks occur when Akshi is opened, resumed, refreshed, or updated. Akshi is a
renewal and configuration companion, not a real-time availability monitor.
